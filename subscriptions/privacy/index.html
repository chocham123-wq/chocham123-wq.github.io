Privacy Policy – Subscription Manager & Bills

Effective date: November 4, 2026

Subscription Manager & Bills ("the app", "we", "us") helps you keep track of your subscriptions and bills, with reminders before each payment. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

No bank login, no account

The app never asks for your bank, card or payment-account credentials and never connects to your bank. It has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts or photos, and we do not run servers that receive your data.

Data stored only on your device

Everything you enter (service names, prices, currencies, billing dates, free-trial dates, payment-method notes such as "Visa •• 4821", links and notes) and your settings are saved locally on your device. This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Delete all data, by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling the app.

Please do not type full card numbers, passwords or other secrets into the notes fields: the app does not need them.

Reminders

Payment, bill and free-trial reminders are scheduled on your device by Android. They are not sent through our servers or any notification service. You can turn notifications off in the app's settings or in your Android settings.

Backups and exports

When you choose Back up or Export to CSV, the app creates a file and opens the Android share menu. The file goes only where you choose to send or save it (for example your Drive, email or phone storage). When you restore a backup, the app reads the file you select on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system, app interactions with ads, and approximate location derived from your IP address. The subscriptions and bills you enter are never shared with Google or any advertiser. Google uses ad data as described in its policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. In other regions, you can limit ad personalization or reset your advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Links to other services

Cancel guides can open the account page of a service, Google Play subscriptions, or a web search in your browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies. The app is not affiliated with the services it lists; their names are used only so you can identify your own subscriptions.

Contacting us

If you email us (for example with Settings → Contact & suggestions), we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply and to improve the app, and we do not sell or share it.

Children

The app is a personal finance tool for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising data processed by Google AdMob described above, the app does not share data with third parties.

Security

Your list stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security. Data exchanged with Google's ad services is encrypted in transit.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will post the new version on this page and change the effective date above.
