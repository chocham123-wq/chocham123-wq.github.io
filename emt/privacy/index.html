Privacy Policy – EMT Prep Coach

Effective date: October 16, 2026

EMT Prep Coach ("the app", "we", "us") is a study app for the NREMT EMT exam. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. The app is not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Registry of Emergency Medical Technicians (NREMT).

Information we do not collect

The app has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or health information, and we do not run our own servers that receive your data.

Data stored only on your device

Your answers, scores, flagged questions, study streak, unfinished quizzes, and reminder settings are saved locally on your phone so you can resume where you left off. This data is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset all progress or by uninstalling the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system, app interactions with ads, and approximate location derived from your IP address. Google uses this information as described in its policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. In other regions, you can limit ad personalization or reset your advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Notifications

If you turn on the daily study reminder, the notification is scheduled locally on your device. No data is sent to us. The reminder is off by default and can be turned off at any time.

Contacting us

If you email us (for example by tapping "Report a problem" on a question), we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply and to fix question content, and we do not sell or share it.

Children

The app is intended for adults preparing for EMT certification and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising data processed by Google AdMob described above, the app does not share data with third parties.

Security

Study progress stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security. Data exchanged with Google's ad services is encrypted in transit.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will post the new version on this page and change the effective date above.
