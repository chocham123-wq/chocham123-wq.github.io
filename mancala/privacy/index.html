Privacy Policy – Mancala: Offline Board Game

Effective date: October 24, 2026

Mancala: Offline Board Game ("the app", "we", "us") is a board game you play against the computer or with a friend on the same device. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Information we do not collect

The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no online multiplayer. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or photos, and we do not run our own servers that receive your data.

Data stored only on your device

Your unfinished game, settings (sound, vibration, speed, board style), statistics (wins, streaks, best score) and puzzle progress are saved locally on your device so you can continue later. This data is not sent to us. You can delete it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system, app interactions with ads, and approximate location derived from your IP address. Google uses this information as described in its policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. In other regions, you can limit ad personalization or reset your advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Contacting us

If you email us (for example with Settings → Send feedback), we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply and to improve the app, and we do not sell or share it.

Children

The app is a general-audience game for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising data processed by Google AdMob described above, the app does not share data with third parties.

Security

Game data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security. Data exchanged with Google's ad services is encrypted in transit.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will post the new version on this page and change the effective date above.
